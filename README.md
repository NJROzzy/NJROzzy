## What I'm Into

### 🦾 Robotics Sim & RL
Multi-task robotic simulation environments in MuJoCo modeling push-dynamics and forward kinematics. DDPG and A3C agents compared for continuous control across convergence, reward shaping, and policy robustness.

`Python` `MuJoCo` `DDPG` `A3C`

### 🎯 Imitation Learning (ACT)
Action Chunking Transformer for contact-rich peg-in-hole insertion — a UR10 arm trained in MuJoCo from expert demos, no RL reward signal. Multi-view RGB + pose + joint-state observation pipeline hit sub-millimeter precision.

`PyTorch` `MuJoCo` `Imitation Learning`
